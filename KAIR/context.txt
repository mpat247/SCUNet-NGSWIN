why the outputs collapse / look same (most likely causes)

domain gap in the artifact simulator

if the synthetic metal shapes are simple circles/ellipses at a few fixed intensities/positions, the net can memorize their average imprint → similar outputs everywhere.

unintended data leakage where the same H is used for many L (e.g., a constant gt.h5 per folder). Your “same-ish output” smell is consistent with this.

no outside-metal constraint

the model can change everything to reduce L1. Without a mask penalty, anatomy outside the trace drifts.
